# Breath of the Wild Multiworld Setup Guide

**Setup** · [In the game](ingame_en.md) · [Options](en_Breath%20of%20the%20Wild.md) · [Hosting](hosting_en.md) ·
[Trackers](poptracker_en.md) · [Mods](mods_en.md) · [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting_en.md)

This guide gets you from nothing to playing Breath of the Wild in an Archipelago room, on Windows, in Cemu. Hosting
the room is on [its own page](hosting_en.md).

## What you need

- [ ] Windows 10 or 11.
- [ ] [Cemu](https://cemu.info/) 2.x.
- [ ] Breath of the Wild for **Wii U**, European (EUR), American (USA) or Japanese (JPN), from your own console, in
      Cemu's game list.
- [ ] Its **update v208** (1.5.0) and its **DLC**, installed in Cemu (File > Install game title, update or DLC).
- [ ] The game started once in Cemu.
- [ ] About 5 GB of free disk space, and 140 MB more for each seed.
- [ ] Your **slot file** (`.apbotw`): on the room's page, "Download Patch File..." next to your name.

> [!NOTE]
> The Switch version is not supported: the integration is built for the Wii U v208 executable. With several versions
> in Cemu, the launcher plays the European one, else the American one.

## Install

1. Download `BOTW-Archipelago-<version>-Windows.zip` from the
   [releases](https://github.com/EIdras/BOTW-Archipelago/releases), in the release's **Assets** (not "Source code").
2. Right-click the zip > Properties, tick **Unblock**, OK. Otherwise Windows asks to confirm each program the first
   time it runs.
3. Unzip it where you like, your Documents for instance (not in Program Files).

Nothing else to install: the release brings its own Python.

## Your first session

Start `BOTW Archipelago.cmd`, or drop your slot file on it. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose
"More info", then "Run anyway".

<!-- ![The launcher's window](../../docs/images/launcher.png) -->

1. **Cemu.** The first time, a window asks where `Cemu.exe` is. The launcher checks the game, its update and the DLC,
   then copies Cemu and your saves into a profile of its own (about 5 GB). Your Cemu installation is never modified.
2. **The seed.** Choose your slot file. The launcher builds the seed's game pack, in about a minute.
3. **The room.** Its address (`archipelago.gg:38281`) is already filled in when your slot file comes from the room's
   page: press Enter. Then type the room's password, or Enter if it has none.
4. **The tracker.** The launcher offers, once, to build a map tracker for PopTracker. It is optional: see
   [Trackers](poptracker_en.md).

Cemu then starts with the seed's game pack, and the Archipelago client runs in the launcher's window.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Start a new game** for a new seed: a save is bound to the seed it was started with.

Play normally: your items arrive in the game, and the Archipelago journal, top right of the screen, says what you
send and receive. [In the game](ingame_en.md) shows what to expect.

Close Cemu to end the session, and leave the launcher's window open until then.

## The next sessions

The launcher lists the seeds already prepared, the last played first. Pick one, press Enter at the room's address
(it is remembered for the seed), and play.

To remove a seed you no longer play, pick **delete a seed** in that list: it deletes the seed and its game pack, and
keeps your saves.

## Chat and commands

Lines typed in the launcher's window go to the room as chat. Archipelago's commands work too: `/hint <item>`,
`/players`, `/status`... are sent to the room as `!hint`, `!players`, `!status`.

## Updating

Download the new release and unzip it, in place of the old one or anywhere else. Your profile, your seeds and their
saves are kept in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BOTW Archipelago`, whatever folder the release is in. The first time you play a
seed with the new version, the launcher builds its game pack again.

> [!WARNING]
> A release does not play every seed: one generated by a world too different from its own is refused, and the
> launcher names the version that generated it. Keep that release to finish the seed. When a seed comes from a newer
> version it can play, the launcher suggests updating.

Give the host the `botw.apworld` of the same release for the next seeds.

## Uninstalling

Delete the release's folder, and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BOTW Archipelago`: the profile, with the saves of your Archipelago
games, and the seeds. Your Cemu installation was never modified.
